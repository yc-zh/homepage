I am an undergraduate student at the School of Informatics, Xiamen University. I am expected to graduate in 2023 and continue my studies for a Master's degree at Xiamen University. My primary research interests include computer vision and machine learning, with a specific focus on medical image processing and deep learning techniques for image segmentation.

In the past few years, I have been involved in research projects related to automated image analysis for oral health, and I am currently working on improving the performance of weakly-supervised learning models for medical image recognition. I also explore applications of Vision Transformers and Multiple Instance Learning to enhance model accuracy in detecting and segmenting anatomical structures in medical images.
Research Interests

My research focuses on developing novel methods for medical image analysis, particularly in the areas of:

  • Weakly-supervised learning techniques for image segmentation.
  • Application of Vision Transformers (ViT) in medical image processing.
  • Development of automated systems for detecting tooth-marked tongues in clinical images.
  • Exploring deep learning methods for 3D tooth instance segmentation from CBCT scans.
