About Me

I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Xiamen University, focusing on computer vision and medical image analysis. My research involves weakly-supervised learning, Vision Transformers, and 3D image segmentation for clinical applications. I aim to contribute to automated systems that improve diagnostic accuracy in medical imaging.
